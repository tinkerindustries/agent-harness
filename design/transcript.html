What is proposed here

  • The sub-turn becomes the unit. Reasoning, assistant text, tool calls and their results live in one card; the usage figures move into the card header. The current page emits those as four sibling blocks, which is most of why a 142-turn session is 86,674 pixels tall.
  • The rail is the browsing affordance. Sub-turns group under the plan item that was in_progress when they ran — a boundary the fold already sees, because every TodoWrite call marks one. Tool glyphs make a turn's shape readable without opening it.
  • Compact density collapses every sub-turn to its header line, so 142 turns fit in two screens. Failing results open regardless of density.
  • A tool call shows its target, not its JSON. Edit shows the path and the +n −n; the arguments blob stays behind the disclosure.
  • Collapsing shrinks mounted DOM, which is a plausible frame-budget win and an unmeasured one. Re-measure with src/perf before claiming it — see web/CLAUDE.md.
