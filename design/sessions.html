What is proposed here

  • In-flight sessions leave the table and become collapsible cards. Collapsed, a card answers "what is it doing, how far in is it"; expanded, it shows the full plan and the last few tool calls without leaving the list.
  • OK splits into DONE, GAVE UP and STOPPED. The three outcomes are already distinct in the store — see components.html for the mapping — and collapsing them into one green badge is what makes every row look successful.
  • The finished table gains a one-line subtitle per row: plan completion and the model's own summary, so scanning does not require opening each transcript.
  • Nothing here starts, steers or stops a run. The disclosure, the filters and the search are all client-side over data the list feed already carries, plus two new fields (complete_status and the live plan).
