What changed from the phase 3 design and why

  • Shared top nav. One .topnav (tokens.css), the same on all four screens — Sessions, Operations, Settings, each a real link with an active state, plus whatever is page-specific pinned right. Today Settings and Operations are each one click from the list and zero clicks from each other or from a transcript; see nav.html for the four states.
  • Fixed: the finished table's own overflow bug. Measured against the running instance — at 1600px wide the table shows only Status and Session; Model, Elapsed, Sub-turns, Cache and Cost are all scrolled off the right edge, because .sess-cell has no max-width and a long plan summary stretches the column until everything after it is pushed out. .sess-cell now caps at 360px with a hard ellipsis, and Elapsed and Cost move to sit right after Session, so the two numbers this pass was asked to prioritise are never the ones that scroll away.
  • Elapsed and cost read as the primary numbers everywhere a session appears — bold and a step larger in both the in-flight card's stat row and the finished table's columns, with sub-turns and cache demoted to secondary weight. They were already being shown; this only changes which ones are loud, matching "what is it doing, how long has it run, what did it cost, what's its status" as the four things worth a glance.
  • New stat strip. Running (of the pool's slots), spend today, median duration today, and done-vs-gave-up today — the per-row numbers rolled up so the fleet-level answer to "how are we doing" doesn't require reading every row. All four are computed client-side over the session list the table already has, plus worker.pool_size off the settings the settings screen already fetches; no new session-row field.
  • Everything from the phase 3 pass is unchanged otherwise: in-flight sessions as collapsible plan cards, DONE/GAVE UP/STOPPED in place of one green OK, the finished table's plan-ratio-and-summary subtitle. Nothing here starts, steers or stops a run.
